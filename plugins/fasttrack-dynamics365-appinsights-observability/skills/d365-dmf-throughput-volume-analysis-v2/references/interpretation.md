# Throughput and volume interpretation

- **FACT:** observed completed counts, execution totals, completion rates, or paired efficiency.
- **CORRELATED:** volume or rate change overlaps another operational signal.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** upstream feed failure, demand change, scheduling change, or performance cause requiring independent evidence.

Volume growth and decline are neutral until business expectations are known. A disappeared material workload may indicate a failed feed, but confirm scheduling and source-system behavior. Poor records-per-second efficiency is stronger evidence of a performance issue than high volume alone.
